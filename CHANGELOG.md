# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-23
- Portal watches the ports your own processes are listening on and shows your local dev servers on Droppy's shelf and in the notch.
- Open a server in your browser, copy its address, or hand it off to your phone with a QR code.
- Get notified in the notch when a dev server starts or stops.
- See everything from the menu bar, too.
